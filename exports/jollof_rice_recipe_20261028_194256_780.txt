Recipe: Jollof Rice
Category: Main Course
Preparation time: 15 minutes
Cooking time: 45 minutes

Ingredients:
- 2 cups long-grain rice
- 3 whole tomatoes, blended
- 1 whole red bell pepper
- 1 whole onion
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2 cups stock
- 1 teaspoon curry powder
- 1 to taste salt

Instructions:
1. Blend tomatoes, bell pepper, and half the onion.
2. Fry chopped onion and tomato paste in a little oil.
3. Add blended mixture and seasonings; cook until thickened.
4. Add rice and stock, cover, and cook until tender, stirring occasionally.
